Our Story

Rooted in faith, built by community

Abu Huraira Islamic Center began when local congregations came together with a shared dream: a large, welcoming Islamic center for the growing Muslim community of the Twin Cities.

After years of fundraising, the community purchased the former Medtronic headquarters on Old Highway 8 in 2012 and named it after Abu Huraira (may Allah be pleased with him), the beloved companion of the Prophet ﷺ known for preserving thousands of hadith.

Today the center is a place to pray, learn, and serve, with plenty of space and parking for families even on the busiest winter Fridays.

First Time Here?

Visiting the Masjid

Whether you are a Muslim new to the area or a neighbor curious about Islam, we would love to welcome you.

Park for free

Our large lot at 3055 Old Hwy 8 has plenty of space, even on busy Fridays and in winter.

Dress modestly

Loose, modest clothing is appreciated. Sisters are asked to cover their hair; scarves are available at the entrance.

Remove your shoes

Shoe racks are provided at the entrance to keep the prayer hall clean.

Say salaam

Our volunteers and imam are happy to show you around and answer any questions.

Groups, schools and interfaith visits

We welcome school classes, churches, community groups and anyone wanting to learn about Islam. Call us to arrange a guided tour and Q&A with the imam.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find your answer? Give us a call at (612) 886-3525.

Is there a prayer space for sisters?

Yes. Sisters have their own dedicated prayer area and wudu facilities, and are welcome at all daily prayers, Jumu'ah and programs.

Can non Muslims visit the masjid?

Absolutely. Visitors of all faiths are welcome. We simply ask that you dress modestly and remove your shoes in the prayer hall. Call ahead if you would like a guided tour.

How do I enroll my child in Quran or weekend school?

Call the masjid office or stop by after any prayer to speak with an administrator. Enrollment typically opens before the start of each semester.

Can I perform my nikah at the masjid?

Yes. Contact us to schedule a nikah with the imam. Please have your state marriage license ready, as required by Minnesota law.

How can I pay my zakat through the masjid?

You can give zakat in person at the masjid or contact the office. Zakat donations are kept separate and distributed only to those eligible to receive it.
